**JSPS Postdoctoral Fellow**, [Yokohama National University](https://www.ynu.ac.jp/english/)

Welcome to my homepage. I work in graph theory and combinatorics, with particular interests in extremal problems related to cycles and paths in structural graph theory.

I joined Central China Normal University as a lecturer in July 2024. Since March 2025, I have been a JSPS International Research Fellow at Yokohama National University, working mainly with Kenta Ozeki.

I’m always happy to discuss mathematics and exchange ideas. Feel free to [email me](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).

## Research interests {#research}

- **Hamiltonian graph theory.** Hamiltonian cycles and paths, Hamiltonian-connectedness, and sufficient conditions involving degree, connectivity, and domination.
- **Extremal graph theory.** Extremal problems involving cycles, paths, matchings, and linear forests, including sharp bounds on the number of edges and cliques and the characterization of extremal graphs.
- **Structural graph theory.** Graph connectivity, vertex cuts, and local structure, with particular interest in claw-free graphs and line graphs of graphs and 3-hypergraphs.
