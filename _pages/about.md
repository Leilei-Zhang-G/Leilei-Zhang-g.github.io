Hello, thank you for visiting my website ! My name is Leilei Zhang (张雷雷), a research fellow in the Faculty of Environment and Information Sciences, Yokohama National University. (Mentor: Prof. Kenta Ozeki).

I joined Central China Normal University as a [lecturer](https://maths.ccnu.edu.cn/info/2011/38761.htm) in July 2024. Since March 2025, I have been a JSPS International Research Fellow at Yokohama National University, working mainly with Kenta Ozeki. I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Mathematics at East China Normal University in 2024 under the supervision of Professor [Xingzhi Zhan](https://math.ecnu.edu.cn/zhan/intro.html). For more information please see my [Curriculum Vitae]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).

I work in graph theory and combinatorics, with particular interests in extremal problems related to cycles and paths in structural graph theory. I’m always happy to discuss mathematics and exchange ideas. Feel free to [email me](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).
