I work in graph theory and combinatorics, with particular interests in extremal problems related to cycles and paths in structural graph theory.

I joined Central China Normal University as a lecturer in July 2024. Since March 2025, I have been a JSPS International Research Fellow at Yokohama National University, working mainly with Kenta Ozeki. I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Mathematics at East China Normal University in 2024 under the supervision of Professor Xingzhi Zhan. For more information please see my [Curriculum Vitae]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).

I’m always happy to discuss mathematics and exchange ideas. Feel free to [email me](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).
